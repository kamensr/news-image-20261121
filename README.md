Entry for [news-image-20261121](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261121):
one 1024×1024 poster summing up the world news of Saturday 21 November
2026. Four illustrated features lead the day — the Kryvyi Rih triple-tap
strike, the Hormuz tanker attacks and the US reply, COP31 in overtime,
and the capture of FBI most-wanted "El Chapo Isidro" — followed by 12
mini-stories, a pinned world map, a UTC timeline, and 33 more one-line
stories, for 49 stories across 7 regions. Every story happened, or was
first reported, on 21 November 2026 in some time zone; a date-check box
names the near-miss stories deliberately excluded. Flat illustration
drawn with PIL — no photographs. `main.py` (standard library only)
copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
